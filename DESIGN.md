# Maverick design system

Menus follow the user-approved screens in `example/Design.html` (September 27, 2026): Home, Select Mode, Hangar, Hangar Armament, Campaign Map and PVE Setup. The aircraft fills the screen and every piece of text floats over the scene. There are no panels, rails or cards with solid fills. Screen rules and navigation live in `docs/game-design/20-game-shell.md`; hangar content lives in `06-hangar.md`.

- Palette: scene black #050709 and #0b0f12; primary text #dae2e6; secondary #a4b0b6; muted/labels #87949b; hairline rgba(174,201,211,0.22); locked #59656b. Cool cyan #9ad7e8 marks selection, focus and the primary action. Amber #ffd08a is reserved for caution and red #ff8a80 for failure. No neon, bloom or decorative glow.
- Type: Barlow Condensed for aircraft identity, headings, labels and buttons (uppercase, tracked); Barlow for body copy; IBM Plex Mono for hotkey hints and tabular readouts. Barlow has no Thai glyphs, so Thai copy uses a Thai face of matching weight with the system Thai font as fallback, with line height that keeps tone marks intact. Controls at least 13px, secondary copy at least 12px (the mock's 11px hints round up to 12px).
- Legibility over the model: a radial vignette (transparent centre to about rgba(2,3,5,0.9) at the edges) and, where needed, a soft edge gradient behind a text column. Never a solid plate or a glow. Text must pass contrast against the brightest aircraft livery at every camera preset.
- Layout: aircraft centred and large; identity and details at the left edge; mode, hangar and play actions at the bottom right, with Play as the one primary button; breadcrumb and hotkey hints at the top left; aircraft or weapon selectors along the bottom. On narrow windows the overlay columns stack under the scene and scroll naturally.
- Selection: a 1–2px cyan rule or outline plus a text state (`● CURRENT`, `AVAILABLE`, `SET ACTIVE`), with aria-pressed or aria-current. Locked items use a dashed outline, muted text and a reason (`REQUIRES M03 + M04`). Status never relies on colour alone. Focus uses a visible 2px ring. Minimum 40px targets.
- Stat bars: thin hairline tracks with a cyan fill, the value in mono, and a tick for the compared aircraft. Values not yet tuned say so (`VALUES PENDING P5 TUNING`) instead of showing invented numbers.
- Motion: one shared menu scene. Routes change the camera preset (Home hero turntable, Select Mode dolly back with the scene dimmed to about 40%, Hangar orbit, Armament bay opening). Demand rendering when idle. Reduced motion removes the turntable, camera transitions and damping. Stage animation travels to an endpoint and holds; turning it off reverses from the current position.
- States: loading, failed asset with retry, WebGL unavailable and no-clips remain inside the screen that needs them. Selection, language and Back stay available during loading or failure. Options the game cannot run yet stay visible with a reason (READY / PLANNED / LOCKED) instead of disappearing.

## Flight HUD

The flight surface follows the reference implementation's glass (example/F22, `hud.js`), ported to `src/features/flight/hudPainter.ts`: one canvas of phosphor green (#62ff84) with 0.66 / 0.84 / full brightness tiers, a translucent green bloom pass under every stroke and glyph, no fills and no CSS filter. Geometry is proportional to a half-frame of min(42% width, 72% height). A wide heading tape with its readout and caret above sits high; the world-registered pitch ladder (rungs every 5°, dashed below the horizon, labels rotated onto the rung), the nose cross and the flight path marker share the centre. Speed (ARCADE km/h, with the PSM entry band as a bracket and engine power beneath) and altitude (metres AGL) tapes stand outboard with pointing value boxes; the A/B reserve column and V/S bar hang outside them. GND and EDGE, pitch/bank and α/G/PSM/HI-G/BRAKE form the status block under the sightline. HTML keeps only dark 2–3px plates: identity, pause, lesson, Flight Lab, and one advisory plate below the status block (beside it in short windows). The glass is redrawn from the interpolated pose every frame; React renders only the canvas and a visually hidden readout. Status uses text as well as brightness; urgent warnings blink on a dark plate. The heading convention is +X north / +Z east. Altitude is metres above the flat training ground; speed explicitly retains the game's ARCADE km/h scale. Menus use the overlay system above.
